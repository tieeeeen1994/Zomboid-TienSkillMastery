title=Tien's Skill Mastery
description=Skills keep growing past level 10. A skill at level 10 earns [b]mastery[/b] from experience: mastery levels 1 to 10, each giving a tenth of small bonuses made for that skill. Mastery 10 is about a fifth better than skill level 10, without breaking anything. Past mastery 10, experience keeps counting as [b]prestige[/b].
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Experience only.[/b] Mastery grows from the experience you earn by using the skill. Reading never counts: a skill book's multiplier is taken back out, and Skill Journal restores are not counted.
description=[*][b]Growing cost.[/b] Each mastery level costs 5% more than the step before it, starting from what the skill's level 10 cost (9000 XP for most skills, 150000 for Strength and Fitness). For most skills mastery 1 costs 9500, mastery 10 costs 14700, and all ten together 119000 XP. Costs are rounded to whole hundreds.
description=[*][b]Level-ups like vanilla's.[/b] Every mastery level shows the "+1 Axe Mastery" halo with the green arrow (+1 Prestige past 10) and plays vanilla's level-up sound.
description=[*][b]Strength and Fitness[/b] lose mastery before they lose a level when you stop training.
description=[*][b]Mastery belongs to the character.[/b] It is gone when the character dies.
description=[*][b]Mastery tab[/b] next to Skills in the character window, laid out like the Skills tab: ten boxes per skill (the next one fills as you progress, hover for the XP), categories you can collapse, and prestige shown as +n. Click a skill to see its bonuses now and at mastery 10.
description=[*][b]What your skills do.[/b] Click a skill's name in the vanilla Skills tab to see what its current level gives in vanilla: every effect with its number, and the features later levels unlock. Click again to close.
description=[*][b]Admin cheats.[/b] Right-click a skill in the Mastery tab to add or remove mastery XP, set the mastery level or clear it. Right-click a skill's name in the Skills tab to add or remove its real XP or set its level. Both work on your own character: in multiplayer for roles allowed to add XP (as for /addxp, checked on the server), in single player with -debug only. XP from /addxp still counts toward mastery, but only 240 of each award gets through.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer.[/b] Mastery is kept on the server and works on dedicated servers. Your skill panel, Skill Journal and other mods still see level 10 skills as full.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Bonuses at mastery 10[/h2]
description=Each mastery level gives a tenth. Plain numbers without % are percentage points on a chance.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fitness:[/b] attack speed +5%, endurance used -10%.
description=[*][b]Strength:[/b] melee damage +10%, knockback +15%, carry weight +3.
description=[*][b]Sprinting:[/b] endurance used while running or sprinting -10%.
description=[*][b]Nimble:[/b] strafe speed while aiming +15% (multiplayer only: single player rewrites it every frame). In multiplayer it also fixes strafe speed lagging up to 2 s behind the server, for everyone.
description=[*][b]Axe, Long Blunt, Short Blunt, Long Blade, Short Blade, Spear[/b] (with a weapon of that skill): damage +10%, attack speed +5%, 5% chance to reroll a hit that did not crit (the reroll uses vanilla's own crit chance; knife stabs keep vanilla's rule: they only crit on close kills).
description=[*][b]Axe, Long Blunt, Spear[/b] also: reach +10%, endurance used swinging -15%. [b]Axe[/b] also: knockback +20%, and a 10% crit reroll chance instead of 5%.
description=[*][b]Maintenance:[/b] 20% chance to undo each point of wear on the weapon in your hand.
description=[*][b]Aiming:[/b] hit chance +5, critical hit chance +5, range +10% (kept inside what the server's anti-cheat allows), aim-in speed +15%.
description=[*][b]Reloading:[/b] reload and rack speed +15%.
description=[*][b]Every crafting skill[/b] (Carpentry, Carving, Cooking, Electrical, Glassmaking, Knapping, Masonry, Metalworking, Mechanics, Pottery, Tailoring, Welding): crafting time -15% on its recipes, dismantling return chance +10.
description=[*][b]Carpentry:[/b] built structure health +20%, unbarricading time -15%, window break chance when picking up -5.
description=[*][b]Carving:[/b] carved spear condition +2.
description=[*][b]Cooking:[/b] nutrition from added ingredients +15%, time to add ingredients -15%.
description=[*][b]Electrical:[/b] hotwire success chance +10, hotwiring time -15%, generator repair +1 condition, 25% chance of an extra electronics scrap when dismantling electronics.
description=[*][b]First Aid:[/b] treatment time -15%, treatment strength +15% (bandages, poultices, splints, stitches, bullet removal).
description=[*][b]Metalworking:[/b] 20% chance to undo each point of tool wear on its recipes.
description=[*][b]Mechanics:[/b] part install and uninstall time -15%, engine repair +1 condition per part, engine parts salvaged +20%.
description=[*][b]Tailoring:[/b] patching time -15%, fabric recovery chance +10, +1 material from ripping clothes, +1 aramid thread use.
description=[*][b]Welding:[/b] burnt car salvage time -15%, one more salvage roll per burnt car, break chance when picking up metal furniture -5.
description=[*][b]Fishing:[/b] line tension limit +15%, natural bait kept +10, big fish +10, junk catches -20%, trophy fish +2.
