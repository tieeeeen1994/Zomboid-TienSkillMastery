title=Tien's Skill Mastery
description=Skills stay vanilla, levels 0 to 10. On top of them come [b]masteries[/b]: named groups of skills, such as [b]Firearms[/b] (Aiming and Reloading) or [b]Construction[/b] (Carpentry and Masonry), each with effects of its own. Once every skill in a mastery is at level 10 you can pick it. A character holds at most [b]three[/b] masteries, so choose what your survivor is known for.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Picks are permanent.[/b] Once three are taken, every other mastery is closed for that character. An admin can remove a pick (its progress goes with it).
description=[*][b]Experience only.[/b] A picked mastery grows from the experience you earn in its skills after you pick it: mastery levels 1 to 10, each giving a tenth of its effects, then endless [b]prestige[/b]. Reading never counts: a skill book's multiplier is taken back out, and Skill Journal restores are not counted.
description=[*][b]Growing cost.[/b] One mastery level is about one level-10 step of any of its skills (9000 XP for most skills, 150000 for Strength and Fitness), and each level costs 5% more than the one before.
description=[*][b]Shared skills.[/b] A skill can be in several masteries (Strength is in three). Experience in it counts for every picked mastery that lists it.
description=[*][b]Paused, not lost.[/b] If a skill in a picked mastery drops below 10 (Strength or Fitness decay), the mastery pauses until the skill is back, keeping its progress and its slot. Strength and Fitness lose mastery points before they lose a level.
description=[*][b]Level-ups like vanilla's.[/b] Every mastery level shows the "+1 Firearms Mastery" halo with the green arrow (+1 Prestige past 10) and plays vanilla's level-up sound.
description=[*][b]Mastery belongs to the character.[/b] It is gone when the character dies.
description=[*][b]Mastery tab[/b] next to Skills in the character window, laid out like the Skills tab: the masteries by group, each with its skills (green once at 10), a Pick button when it is ready, ten boxes once picked (hover for the points). Click a mastery to see its effects now and at mastery 10.
description=[*][b]What your skills do.[/b] Click a skill's name in the vanilla Skills tab to see what its current level gives in vanilla: every effect with its number, and the features later levels unlock. Click again to close.
description=[*][b]Admin cheats.[/b] Right-click a picked mastery in the Mastery tab to change its points or level, or remove the pick; right-click a skill's name in the Skills tab to change its XP or level. Own character only: in multiplayer for roles allowed to add XP, in single player with -debug.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer.[/b] Masteries are kept on the server and work on dedicated servers. Your skill panel, Skill Journal and other mods still see level 10 skills as full.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Signature abilities[/h2]
description=Each mastery also unlocks a signature ability, in full from the moment it is picked (it does not grow with mastery levels).
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Firearms:[/b] quickdraw (one-handed guns drawn and holstered 4 times faster) and run-and-gun reloading (reloading does not stop when you run).
description=[*][b]Bludgeoning:[/b] push back: every swing with a long blunt weapon also staggers back the zombies in front of you that it did not hit, for a little endurance each.
description=[*][b]Swordsmanship:[/b] hamstring: sneaking and aiming with a long blade, every hit leaves the zombie crawling.
description=[*][b]Street Fighting:[/b] bowl them over: sprinting into a zombie knocks it down instead of tripping you, for a little endurance each (no endurance moodle).
description=[*][b]Axemanship:[/b] in combat stance, the throw key (Space, rebindable) throws a one-handed axe at the zombie under the cursor's head, for half again its damage: the one in your hand, else one loose in your main inventory, then one on your hotbar, never from bags. Gravity pulls it down on the way: head hits (triple damage, as vanilla's) out to about 4 tiles, then the body, and past about 11 tiles it drops short. It lands where it stops. The aim reticle shows whenever a throw is possible.
description=[*][b]Spearcraft:[/b] the same key throws the spear in your hand: head hits out to about 4 tiles, reach about 10.
description=[*][b]Knifework:[/b] the lunge: jaw stabs reach half again as far, and you close the gap as you stab.
description=[*][b]Conditioning:[/b] Heavy Load penalties count one level lower (combat speed, endurance used, recovery).
description=[*][b]Smithing:[/b] masterwork: weapons and clothing you forge ignore 15% of their wear, whoever uses them.
description=[*][b]Construction:[/b] half the time a 4th plank from each sawn log, 12 stones instead of 10 from a large stone.
description=[*][b]Handicraft:[/b] deft hands: every timed action 10% faster.
description=[*][b]Engineering:[/b] engine overhaul: Engine Parts raise a car's engine quality, one point each up to 100 (easier starts, less fuel, full power).
description=[*][b]Salvaging:[/b] scrap any car, not only burnt wrecks (nobody inside, everything taken out, not towing, not in someone else's safehouse), and cut vehicle parts such as doors, hoods and seats into metal.
description=[*][b]Mending:[/b] restore: clear an item's repair count for the price of a repair, so it repairs like new again.
description=[*][b]Field Medicine:[/b] field triage: "Tend wounds" on yourself or someone next to you queues every needed treatment at once: glass and bullets out, fractures splinted, wounds bandaged with the best bandage you carry (theirs too). Infected wounds are left for proper treatment.
description=[*][b]Provisioning:[/b] choice cuts: meat you butcher is 15% more filling and nutritious.
description=[*][b]Hunting:[/b] your traps keep their bait and are never destroyed, animals caught alive last 4 times longer, and a quarter of catches come in twos.
description=[*][b]Homesteading:[/b] tend the field (water, cure and fertilize every plant in a patch you pick) and sow the field (plant every plowed square in it with the seed you choose).
description=[*][b]Angling:[/b] fish finder: aiming a rod at water shows how many fish are there and how much trash.
description=[*][b]Fieldcraft:[/b] search mode is not switched off by zombies or running, and zombies inside the search circle are marked wherever they are.
