title=Tien's Skill Mastery
description=Skills stay vanilla, levels 0 to 10. On top of them come [b]masteries[/b]: named groups of skills, such as [b]Firearms[/b] (Aiming and Reloading) or [b]Construction[/b] (Carpentry and Masonry), each with effects of its own. Once every skill in a mastery is at level 10 you can pick it. A character holds at most [b]three[/b] masteries, so choose what your survivor is known for.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Picks are permanent.[/b] Once three are taken, every other mastery is closed for that character. An admin can remove a pick (its progress goes with it).
description=[*][b]Experience only.[/b] A picked mastery grows from the experience you earn in its skills after you pick it: mastery levels 1 to 10, each giving a tenth of its effects, then endless [b]prestige[/b]. Reading never counts: a skill book's multiplier is taken back out, and Skill Journal restores are not counted.
description=[*][b]Growing cost.[/b] One mastery level is about one level-10 step of any of its skills (9000 XP for most skills, 150000 for Strength and Fitness). Mastery 1 costs 5% more than that step, and each level after it adds another 5% of the step: 105%, 110%, 115% and so on.
description=[*][b]Shared skills.[/b] A skill can be in several masteries (Strength is in three). Experience in it counts for every picked mastery that lists it.
description=[*][b]Paused, not lost.[/b] If a skill in a picked mastery drops below 10 (Strength or Fitness decay), the mastery pauses until the skill is back, keeping its progress and its slot. Strength and Fitness lose mastery points before they lose a level.
description=[*][b]Level-ups like vanilla's.[/b] Every mastery level shows the "+1 Firearms Mastery" halo with the green arrow (+1 Prestige past 10) and plays vanilla's level-up sound.
description=[*][b]Mastery belongs to the character.[/b] It is gone when the character dies.
description=[*][b]Mastery tab[/b] next to Skills in the character window, laid out like the Skills tab: the masteries by group, each with its skills (green once at 10), a Pick button when it is ready (a grey Coming Soon on those not open yet), ten boxes once picked (hover for the points). Click a mastery to see its effects now and at mastery 10. Long lines here and in the Skills tab's effect lists wrap; set the length in Options > Mods.
description=[*][b]What your skills do.[/b] Click a skill's name in the vanilla Skills tab to see what its current level gives in vanilla: every effect with its number, and the features later levels unlock. Click again to close.
description=[*][b]Admin cheats.[/b] Right-click a picked mastery in the Mastery tab to change its points or level, or remove the pick; right-click a skill's name in the Skills tab to change its XP or level, or max every skill. Own character only: in multiplayer for roles allowed to add XP, in single player with -debug.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer.[/b] Masteries are kept on the server and work on dedicated servers. Your skill panel, Skill Journal and other mods still see level 10 skills as full.
description=[/list]
