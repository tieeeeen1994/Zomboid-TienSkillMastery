0.4.5

Firearms: Fire Rate
- The fire rate bonus now applies to single fire only: single shots (and guns with only one fire mode) fire faster, full auto and burst keep their normal rate
- Gunworks guns now really get it in single fire: Gunworks' own fire rate limit ignored the bonus before

Firearms: Running Reload
- Updated for the latest Gunworks and Guns of Marz: the jungle-mag flip is covered, and the lever-action and break-action rack animations match the new ones
- Guns that use Hot Brass's reload animations (its double-barrel, lever-gun and roller-delayed reloads) now keep them on the run too
- Flipping a jungle magazine no longer stops when you start running
